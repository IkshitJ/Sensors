Calibration bench

      
Calibration sketch is running on the entry gate's plates (move the board to the exit gate to do those). Each plate has two bridges (ghat-side and marg-side cell pairs). Put a test weight in the centre of a plate, then in the serial box: t to tare with the plate empty, k20 with 20 kg on it, n for the next plate, a to print every factor.

      
      
      

    

  


  

    

      

        
Accuracy benchmark

        
Runs both gate boards' firmware headless with no hardware: 17 scenarios (pilgrims arriving and leaving, straight and non-linear, tidal flows) × 3 random seeds × 10 simulated minutes, on all CPU cores. The firmware is a black box: its counts come only from what it prints on Serial. Shown: crossings counted on both strips, the entry / exit gate counts, and the error of the "inside the premises" counter at the end of each run.
