Accuracy benchmark

        
Runs the counter firmware headless with no hardware: 9 scenarios × 3 random seeds × 10 simulated minutes, on all CPU cores. The firmware is a black box: its count comes only from what it prints on Serial. Accuracy is shown in total and per direction.
